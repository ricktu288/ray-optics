WebGPU cooperative-ray benchmark

This runs a small multi-bounce 2D ray simulation. It compares one invocation per ray with multiple workgroup lanes or one hardware subgroup per ray. The cooperative paths include closest-hit reduction, synchronization, hit broadcast, reflection, and the next traversal.

Direct variants scan every curve without a BVH. BVH4 cooperative variants divide disjoint BVH subtrees between lanes. All variants execute the same exact ray/segment intersection and reflection code.

Targeted sweeps: Large curve crossover Large ray crossover (BVH4) The curve sweep increases scene size while ray parallelism is scarce. The ray sweep reaches 262,144 active rays and omits direct scanning so that very large, predictably slow Cartesian products do not obscure the BVH crossover.
